INTELLIGENT VEHICLE TRACKING

Reliable vehicle tracking for safer and smarter mobility.

TRPRO 140 is an advanced 2G Vehicle Location Tracking Device (VLTD) designed for real-time vehicle monitoring, passenger safety and efficient fleet management, in compliance with AIS-140 and IS 16833 standards.

By connecting vehicles to centralized monitoring platforms through mobile networks, the solution enables fleet operators to track vehicle movements, review journey history, monitor driving behavior and receive timely alerts from virtually anywhere with network coverage.

Intelligent monitoring capabilities help identify unsafe driving practices, reduce unnecessary vehicle idling and improve fleet productivity. Integrated emergency assistance enables drivers and passengers to raise alerts during critical situations, supporting faster response and enhanced travel safety.

Designed for everyday transportation and commercial fleet operations, the solution provides greater operational visibility, reliable journey information and improved vehicle security, even supporting offline data collection during temporary network interruptions.
