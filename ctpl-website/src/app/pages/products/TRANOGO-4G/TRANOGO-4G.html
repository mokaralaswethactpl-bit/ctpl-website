PRODUCT OVERVIEW

Smarter mobility through intelligent 4G tracking.

TRANOGO-4G is an advanced AIS-140 compliant Vehicle Location Tracking Device (VLTD) designed to enhance vehicle safety, real-time location monitoring and fleet management.

With reliable 4G connectivity and intelligent tracking technology, fleet operators can monitor vehicle movements, review travel routes, analyze driving behavior and receive timely alerts. These capabilities help improve operational efficiency, reduce unnecessary fuel consumption and support informed decision-making.

Advanced monitoring and emergency alert capabilities enhance passenger safety, strengthen vehicle security and enable faster response to unexpected situations. Intelligent data management ensures that valuable tracking information is preserved even during temporary network interruptions.

Designed for public transportation, logistics, commercial fleets and emergency services, the solution enables safer journeys, efficient fleet operations and a more connected transportation ecosystem.
