Vehicle Tracking Device AIS-140 compliant SOS system is a vital tool for enhancing the safety and security of women and girl children across India. Integrated into public and private vehicles, the system features an easily accessible emergency panic button that, when activated, instantly transmits real-time location data to designated State Command and Control Centers and emergency response authorities. This rapid alert mechanism ensures timely intervention during distress situations—such as harassment, abduction, or accidents—providing vulnerable passengers with a reliable lifeline. By leveraging government-mandated telematics standards, CTPL's technology empowers women and girls with greater mobility, confidence, and protection in everyday travel.
